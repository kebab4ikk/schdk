# Checklist for every question

A question goes into the pack only if every line is "так". Fix it, or replace it with a new one.

**Truth**
- [ ] Every fact in the text and the comment was read on a page that was actually opened, and
      that page is in «Джерело».
- [ ] A surprising claim is confirmed by a second source, or the one source is authoritative.
- [ ] Numbers, dates, names and spellings match the source exactly (with «близько» where the
      source rounds).

**Answer**
- [ ] The text leaves only one answer, plus those listed in «Залік».
- [ ] The answer form is stated («Назвіть…», «Напишіть…», «двома словами»…).
- [ ] No word in the text gives the answer away (same root, translation, obvious association).
- [ ] The grammar of the placeholder (gender, number, case) fits the real answer.

**Playability**
- [ ] A team without the fact can reason its way in within a minute: at least one real clue.
- [ ] It isn't a famous ЧГК "баян", and the host has the repeat-check link
      `https://questions.com.ua/search/<відповідь>`.
- [ ] At most ~70 words; it can be understood when read aloud once.
- [ ] Foreign or unusual words have a stress mark or a [вимова] hint; reading instructions are in
      `[Ведучим: …]`.
- [ ] Fits the audience (for школярі: nothing cruel, frightening, adult or partisan).

**Layout**
- [ ] The fields are in order: Запитання N. / text / Відповідь / Залік / Незалік / Коментар /
      Джерело / Автор, with nothing missing that the question needs.
- [ ] The comment explains how the answer comes out, in two or three sentences.

**Within the tour**
- [ ] No other question has the same or a very close answer.
- [ ] No other question gives this one away (or the reverse).
